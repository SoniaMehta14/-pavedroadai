From AI strategy to a standing Center of Excellence.

I work with you from the first strategy conversation until your Center of Excellence runs without me. Every engagement includes fixing what the assessment finds, not only reporting it.

The route

  1. Assess
    First 30 days

    Find where AI creates real value

    I map current AI use across teams, including the pilots already running, rank each by value and risk, and agree a 12 month roadmap with leadership. Every pilot gets a clear call: continue, fix, or stop.

  2. Build
    Days 31 to 90

    Stand up the Center of Excellence and make the fixes

    Charter, operating model, intake, shared platform, and governance. I also do the work the assessment calls for, such as putting a gateway in front of models, adding evaluations, closing data handling gaps, and getting one workflow into production with a named owner.

  3. Scale
    Day 91 onward

    Enable every team

    Role based training, reusable building blocks, and a steady cadence of new use cases moving into production. I step back once your own team runs the Center of Excellence.

What I deliver

AI strategy and roadmap

A clear point of view on where AI fits your business, which use cases come first, and how you will measure return.

Readiness assessment, with the fixes

An inventory of the AI already in use, checked against what is actually running, with each pilot scored for risk and value. The findings come with the work to fix them.

Center of Excellence setup

The team, the charter, the shared platform, and the operating rhythm that turn scattered pilots into a managed portfolio.

Team enablement

Training for developers, architects, and leaders so every function can build and use AI with confidence, not just the engineering team.

AI assurance

Evaluation harnesses, policy as code, and audit trails that show your agents do what they should, built into how software ships rather than added at the end.
