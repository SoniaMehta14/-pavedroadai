AI is everywhere in your company. It is not yet working together.

Pilots without a plan

Every team is experimenting on its own, with its own tools and its own vendors. Few of those pilots ever reach production.

The same work, rebuilt

Teams solve identical problems separately. Prompts, integrations, and lessons learned stay trapped inside one group.

Risk nobody owns

Data handling, model choice, and review standards vary team by team. Leadership cannot see what is running or whether it is safe.

One road, built once, that every team can travel.

A Center of Excellence is the central team that brings AI into the company, proves what works, and feeds it forward to every team. We build that center with you, then hand it over running.

  1. Assess
    First 30 days

    Find where AI creates real value

    We map current AI use across teams, rank opportunities by value and risk, and agree a 12 month roadmap with leadership.

  2. Build
    Days 31 to 90

    Stand up the Center of Excellence

    Charter, operating model, intake for team requests, shared platform, and the governance that lets teams move quickly without guessing.

  3. Scale
    Day 91 onward

    Enable every team

    Role based training for developers, architects, and leaders, reusable building blocks, and a steady cadence of new use cases moving into production.

What we do

AI strategy and roadmap

Center of Excellence setup

What the platform actually looks like.

Not a slide, a real shape. Every AI system we build routes through the same five stages, so nothing reaches a user without being checked, and nothing has to be rebuilt by the next team that needs it.

Built for organizations that answer to regulators.

  • Policy as code and evaluation harnesses built directly into your CI/CD pipeline
  • Provenance and telemetry for every model driven decision
  • Verification gates in your release pipeline before anything reaches users
  • An open maturity model so you can measure progress over time
